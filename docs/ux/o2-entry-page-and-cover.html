Why this is a decision

An entry today has no photograph anywhere in the payload, and the one place the structured layer already lives, the aside, breaks on its own account of it. apps/web/src/lib/components/entry/EntryTabs.svelte:88 fixes that column at md:w-64, 256px, and the five-label strip at :92-93 (class="flex border-b border-line") has no overflow handling at all; each tab at :96 is flex-1 with neither truncate nor min-w-0, so the row is wider than its own column and the last label hangs off the edge. In English "Audit" is short enough to survive; in Italian "Verifica" is not, which is the tell: the layout depends on how long a translated word happens to be, not on a rule that holds for any label.

Nothing about a cover fixes itself either. apps/web/src/routes/w/[universe]/e/[slug]/+page.server.ts:139-164 lists exactly what the page reads: entity fields, relations, facts, history, mentions, proposals, audit flags, media assets, style, generate, and nothing named cover among them. packages/db/src/schema/media.ts:68-98 explains why: media_asset has no primary, cover or role column, so a portrait, a map and three ambient shots an entry might own are all equal rows, returned in creation order by mediaAssetsForEntity (packages/db/src/queries/media.ts:136-142). B1 = C decided the page is a document plus a switching right column, and that is still the right shape for what it was asked to hold. What it never had to answer is a cover, because there were no images when it was drawn, and what it got wrong in the build is a tab strip narrower than its own labels.

Guardrail 1 propose, never apply, one accept per entry. A cover is not exempt from it: whichever surface ends up holding it, an image only becomes the entry's cover the moment a person clicks something that says so, the same rule that already governs every proposal on this page.

Options

A

Cover band, aside loses its tabs

A capped band sits above the title, and the aside stops being a tab strip at all: five collapsible sections stacked instead, each carrying its own count, Relations open by default and Facts closed per B4.

Buys

  • The clipping bug is fixed by construction, not by truncation: there is no strip left to clip.
  • Each section becomes linkable, #facts, which a tab strip never was.
  • A cover reads as the entry's identity the way a banner does, and the sections keep working at 256px in every language.

Costs

  • The aside gets long: on a fuller entry, History is a scroll away rather than a click away.
  • A band steals vertical space above the prose on every entry, including the thin ones, unless it is capped.
  • One aspect ratio cannot flatter both a person and a coastline, at least not as drawn here.
B

Portrait card, tabs become one control

The cover sits at the top of the 256px column, ratio chosen by entity type. The five tabs collapse into a single picker showing the active panel and its count, which is a shadcn-svelte Select per I9 and O4's control to draw, not mine.

Buys

  • The document loses nothing vertically: the portrait sits beside the sentences that describe it.
  • One control cannot clip, and it survives a sixth panel the way five tabs would not.
  • The type-driven ratio, 3:4 for a character, 16:9 for a place, 1:1 for a faction sigil or an item, is the honest answer to a person versus a place.

Costs

  • A picker hides four panels behind a click where tabs showed five at a glance, worse discoverability, and B4's "on demand" does not cover picking one option to hide the rest.
  • 256px is a small cover, especially once the portrait sits above three relation lines and nothing else.
  • A wide landscape image, a place, is cramped at that width.
C

Header object with an icon rail

The cover, title, badges and actions compose into one header block, a band with a scrim carrying the title bottom-left and actions top-right. The aside keeps all five panels, moved onto a vertical icon rail, widened to 320px.

Buys

  • Labels cannot clip because there are none left to clip.
  • The panel gets 64px more room, which Facts and Audit actually need once an entry has more than one of each.
  • A rail scales past five panels the way a strip or a picker both stop doing eventually.

Costs

  • The product has no icon language yet, so this is I9's Lucide dependency arriving as a design decision rather than a library choice.
  • "Facts" and "Audit" have no obvious glyph, and a wrong icon is worse than a clipped word.
  • A tooltip is not a phone: #148's sheet still has to carry text labels, so the rail buys nothing there.
  • Text over an image needs a scrim that survives both palettes, drawn once here and owed to every entry with a cover under G1.

Rejected outright

Generating a cover automatically on create or first view

Tempting because a new entry would never look bare. It breaks Guardrail 1 the moment the image renders as the entry's face before anyone accepted it, and it breaks G11's confirm-every-paid-action and F1's one action, always confirm the spend: a portrait costs 3 credits per SAMPLE-WORLD.md, and nothing here asked first.

Keeping five tabs and shrinking the font or the padding until they fit

Not a fix, a delay. A layout that depends on how long a translated word happens to be will clip again the moment a shorter font stack or a longer locale meets it, German compound nouns run long, and the bug demonstrated above returns under a different label.

Showing a cover on the players' wiki because the entry has one

media_asset.published_to_players (packages/db/src/schema/media.ts:68-98) exists precisely so that Guardrail 6 has no exception for images. A cover the GM never published is still unreviewed content, and the players' wiki does not get to show it just because it happens to sit at the top of the document.

What I would take

A, amended twice

I'd take A, amended twice. Cap the band so it never eats more than about a fifth of the first screenful, and don't draw an empty dashed rectangle when an entry has no cover: a slot that's always empty on every thin entry reads worse than no slot at all, so the absent state drawn above is genuinely what ships, not a placeholder waiting for content. Fold in B's rule about the ratio: the band crops to 21:9 for a place and to something closer to square for a person, positioned rather than stretched, so one shape does not have to flatter both a coastline and a face.

The schema question is separate from the layout one, and I'd answer it now rather than leave it for whoever builds #284. A nullable entity.cover_asset_id with on delete set null, not a role column on media_asset: one cover is a single fact about the entity, and a role column on the asset table invites two rows both claiming role = 'cover' with nothing stopping it. The Images panel grows one new action, use as cover, and that action is also the accept: an image a proposal generated does not become the entry's face until a person presses it, the same click guardrail 1 already asks for everywhere else on the page.

Cheapest way to find out this is wrong: put the capped band on the five entries in the sample world that have no image at all and just read the page. If a capped, empty-safe band still makes a thin entry look unfinished sitting above a short paragraph, the cover belongs in the aside after all, next to the structure rather than above the prose, which is B.

What this locks in

  • #284 and the epic #282 stop meaning "fix the clipping bug": they gain a migration, entity.cover_asset_id, nullable, on delete set null, and a loader field the payload at +page.server.ts:139-164 does not carry today.
  • O1's card grid depends on that same column existing, so O1's thumbnails cannot ship before this migration lands, whichever of O1's own options wins.
  • The Images panel's new action, use as cover, is the accept step: nothing generated becomes a cover without a human click (guardrail 1), and the same action is where published_to_players gets checked before a cover shows on /p/<slug> (guardrail 6).
  • The aside's five panels keep exactly what they hold today, relations, facts, images, history, audit; only what carries them changes here. B4's on-demand default, C9's badge and B3's one-click typing are not reopened.
