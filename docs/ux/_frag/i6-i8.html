I6 · the account and the settings that have no home

Settings, and the account page that does not exist

Five leaf pages hold every account-level control the product has, and a sixth control, the account itself, has no page at all. apps/web/src/routes/settings/* is five unconnected screens, reachable only if you already know the URL, and none of them let you change the name or the email the product prints on every page you visit.

Today

  • No account page. The product prints the user's name and knows their email (apps/web/src/lib/components/shell/AuthStatus.svelte:21,38), and nowhere under apps/web/src/routes/settings/ can either be changed. There is no password change and no delete account.
  • Five islands, no index. routes/settings/keys/+page.svelte:49, export/+page.svelte:15, language/+page.svelte:27, billing/+page.svelte:26 and appearance/+page.svelte:24 each open with the identical "← Universes" link and nothing else in common; there is no routes/settings/+page.svelte to list them.
  • Two of five are reachable at all, and only from one place each. Home links only to appearance (routes/+page.svelte:59-64); the per-universe settings page links to appearance and export (routes/u/[universe]/settings/+page.svelte:26,29). Keys, billing and language have no inbound link anywhere in the app - not even from Sidebar.svelte:69-95, the one nav a signed-in GM actually uses.
  • The sign-in bar is chrome that never leaves, and duplicates itself. routes/+layout.svelte:50 mounts AuthStatus on every route, including inside a universe, where routes/u/[universe]/+layout.svelte:16 then stacks the 256px Sidebar underneath it - so a signed-in GM carries "Signed in as Lorenzo Fiore · Sign out" as a second header row above a sidebar (Sidebar.svelte:49-117) that has no footer of its own to hold it instead.
  • Sign out exists in exactly one place. AuthStatus.svelte:39-46 is the only sign-out control in the product; lose that 40px strip and there is no other way out of a session.

Guardrail 5 the keys page is where the product says which provider sees your campaign content, and /privacy says the rest (F3, the contextual sentence on keys/+page.svelte:55-69 already links there). Whatever holds Settings after this decision has to be reachable in one move from anywhere in the product, or "stated plainly" is only true for a GM who already knows the URL by heart.

Options

Out of scope here on purpose: the per-universe settings page at /u/[universe]/settings, the writing switch and the precedence panel. A2 already gave that nav item to the universe; this decision is only about the account that sits above every universe.

A

A settings index

/settings lists the five leaves as rows, each with the sentence its own page already opens with. No shell, no menu, one new route.

Buys

  • Cheapest of the three: one route, no shell change, no new component.
  • Findable once, then bookmarkable - a GM who visits twice never has to search again.

Costs

  • Leaves the account gap open: still no name, email, password or delete control anywhere.
  • Still zero-click reachable from nowhere; a GM two levels into an entry still has to know /settings exists.
  • The guardrail 5 problem stays unsolved - keys is still a URL, not a move from anywhere.
B

Account menu in the shell, two-pane settings

The shell's own footer carries the signed-in user. Clicking it opens a menu: Account, Language, Appearance, Keys, Billing, Export, Sign out. The five pages become one route with a left sub-nav and the leaves as panes, and Account is a new pane: name, email, password, sign out everywhere, delete account.

Buys

  • The five islands stop being islands: one route, one sub-nav, matching the pattern the per-universe settings page already ships (issue #107).
  • Account exists for the first time - name, email, password and delete account get a home, closing the gap AuthStatus.svelte:38 has printed since issue #86.
  • Sign out and Keys are reachable from the footer on every screen the shell renders, which is what guardrail 5 needs.

Costs

  • Depends on the app shell existing outside a universe at all, which nothing ships today - Sidebar.svelte only mounts inside routes/u/[universe]/+layout.svelte. This option is the settings half of that fix, not the whole of it.
  • One more overlay pattern (the footer menu) that A3's palette does not route through yet.
  • "LF" in a circle is a placeholder mark; the product has no avatar or photo concept to draw on, so the footer invents the smallest possible one.
C

Settings inside the shell's own sidebar

No menu, no separate page: choosing Settings in the account-level nav replaces the main pane with the sub-nav and its panes directly, the way the universe's own settings page already works today.

Buys

  • No popover state to design or build - one interaction pattern, reused from the universe settings page that already ships.
  • Settings is always visible as a row, not discovered by clicking an avatar first.

Costs

  • Puts a "Settings" row in an account-level nav next to the universe list; if that list ever grows past a couple of entries the row competes with A2's own seven-item cap for the same kind of space, even though it is a different list. Worth naming before it is built, not after.
  • Sign out has no dedicated place to live - it is now a click into Settings, then into Account, for something that used to be one button away.
  • The account identity (name) sits in a static footer with nothing to click; the product still has no one place that says "this is you" and lets you act on it.

I would take B. It is the only one of the three that gives the account a page, not just a route, and it collapses five orphaned leaves plus a bar that never leaves into one pattern the per-universe settings page has already proven (issue #107). It costs the most because it is the one that actually needs the app shell to wrap every route, not only universe routes - name that dependency up front rather than discover it mid-build. Once paid, it is what makes guardrail 5 true instead of aspirational: Keys and the link to /privacy are a footer click away from an entry, a proposal queue, or the home page, not a URL someone has to already know.

I8 · empty states

Ten sentences, one shape, and it is the wrong shape for most of them

Every empty list in Canonry renders the same way: one grey sentence, and nothing else. That is correct for some of these and wrong for most, because "nothing pending" and "no universes yet" are not the same kind of empty, and treating all ten identically means the one place a button belongs never gets one.

Today

  • Home, cold start. "No universes yet - yours, or one you were added to, will show up here." (en.ts:956) - the one screen a brand-new account actually needs a button on has none.
  • Universe home, cold start. "Nothing here yet." (en.ts:949) - shown above.
  • Works, cold start, with a half-measure. "No works yet." (en.ts:886) - unlike the two above, this page does carry a create form (routes/u/[universe]/works/+page.svelte:47-53), but it sits below the fold as a full form, not as the one button a cold start wants next to the sentence that names it.
  • Proposals inbox, settled. "Nothing pending. Edit an entry to start a propagation run." (en.ts:386) - correct today: no button, no nag.
  • Review queue, settled. "Nothing left to review." (en.ts:434) - correct today, same reason.
  • Audit, settled. "No flags on this entry right now." (en.ts:277) - correct today, same reason.
  • Relations, derived. "No relations recorded yet." (en.ts:263) - names no dependency; a GM cannot tell whether relations run automatically or need an edit first.
  • Facts, derived. "No facts extracted yet." (en.ts:267) - same gap, and B4 already decided facts are on-demand, so the trigger exists and just is not named here.
  • History, derived. "No revisions yet." (en.ts:271) - same gap.
  • Images, the one accidental exception. "No images yet." (en.ts:288) already sits above a working Generate button (lib/components/media/EntryMediaPanel.svelte:223,242-250) - not because anyone decided cold starts need a primary action, but because F1's always-visible generate control happens to render there regardless. One surface got the right shape by accident; the other two cold starts did not get it at all.

Options

A

One component, three variants

An EmptyState component with three shapes: cold takes a sentence and one primary action, settled takes a sentence and nothing else, derived takes a sentence and an explanation that links to the thing it depends on. Every string still comes from the i18n catalogue - the component takes props, it owns no copy.

Buys

  • Works and home's cold starts finally get the primary button they are missing; relations, facts and history's derived absences finally say what they are waiting on.
  • One place to fix the shape everywhere at once rather than nine call sites drifting further apart, which is what already happened to works and images.
  • Copy stays in messages(locale) exactly like every other string in the product - the component takes sentence, an optional action: {'{'} label, href {'}'}, and an optional explain: {'{'} text, linkLabel, href {'}'}, never English literals.

Costs

  • Three call shapes to learn instead of one paragraph tag; a surface with a real fourth shape (there is not one in the inventory today) has nowhere to go without extending the component.
  • Someone has to decide, once, which of the three each of the ten existing empties is - the audit above does that work, but the next new surface has to do it again.
B

Per-surface, hand-written, kept deliberately

What ships today, kept on purpose rather than by default: no component, a checklist in the review rubric instead.

Buys

  • Zero component to design, build or maintain; each page keeps full control of its own layout.

Costs

  • A checklist is read at review time, not enforced at build time - it is exactly what let works' create form and images' accidental button drift into two different shapes already, with nobody deciding either on purpose.
  • Fixing the three real gaps (home, universe home, works' missing button; relations, facts, history's missing explanation) still means editing six files by hand today, and six more the next time a seventh empty state ships.
C

Illustrated empties

Drawn to reject it. A small line illustration above the sentence, warmer copy underneath.

Buys

  • Warmest tone of the three; might read as friendlier on a first-run empty universe.

Costs

  • No illustration budget and no icon set exist - H2 spent the product's only drawn asset on the mark itself, on the grounds that a raster gets redrawn from exactly one SVG. An empty-state illustration is a second art direction the product has not budgeted for and A1's reading room, paper and serif, was chosen precisely to not need one.
  • A decorative empty state ages the moment anything nearby changes; a doodle is harder to keep current than a sentence.

Guardrail 7 "won't stay that way" promises a populated, coherent future the product cannot certify. Canonry's whole stance is "here is what does not add up," never a promise that it will resolve itself - encouraging copy on an empty state is exactly where that promise starts leaking in.

I would take A. The audit above already sorts all ten existing empties into the three shapes with no leftover case, so the component costs nothing in new taxonomy, only in build. It fixes the two genuine cold-start gaps (home, universe home) and the one half-built one (works) with a real primary action, gives the three derived absences (relations, facts, history) the explanation they are missing, and leaves the three settled states exactly as quiet as they are today. It also folds images' accidental button into the same deliberate shape instead of leaving it as the one page that happens to be right. This locks in the contract for #16 relations, #17/#18 facts and history, #47 the proposal inbox, #55 audit and #20 works, so the next surface to ship an empty list, the players' wiki gap page (E7, #82-85) among them, reuses a decided shape instead of inventing a fourth kind.
