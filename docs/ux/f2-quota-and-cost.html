Why this is a decision

SPEC 15 fixes the shape of the plan: included quota with routing between cheap and premium models, BYO-key available but never the default, and two words that may never appear: "opaque" and "unlimited". It does not fix where the number lives. Get this wrong and one of two bad products ships: a quota nobody ever looks at until an import fails mid-run, or a dashboard so present it turns a wiki into a billing app. The warm cache makes this sharper than ordinary usage metering, because it spends credits on a schedule the GM did not click: on write, on prep, on entering a place, nightly. A budget line that only appears when the GM asks for something directly will never show that spend.

SPEC 15 "Warming has a visible budget line, because it spends the model when nobody is watching and an invisible spend is how a quota loses its meaning." No opaque credits, and never the word unlimited.

Since decided

H1 settled what the meter drawn here actually counts: every number on it comes from operation_price, the same table an admin edits from F5. Generation spends it, at whatever the table currently says for that operation; reading, semantic search, mention suggestions and the retrieval behind an Ask answer, contributes nothing, on every universe, not only a switched-off one. An operation with no row in the table is not a free ride: it fails loudly and names itself, so nothing chargeable ever ships silently priced at zero by omission. The included-quota and warm-budget rows above, and the "Recent operations" list in Option B, are unchanged by this: they were always going to show real numbers, this is only where those numbers now come from.

Options

A

A meter in the shell

A persistent chip near the universe switcher, in credits, with its own row for the warm budget. Always visible, click for the breakdown.

Buys

  • The only design here that can show background spend as it happens, which is the whole point of a warm budget line per SPEC 15.
  • Never a surprise mid-session: the number is one glance away at all times.
  • One place to look, always the same place.

Costs

  • Credits alone are opaque unless the EUR line stays attached and current; divorce the two and this becomes the exact complaint SPEC 15 names.
  • One more thing permanently in shell chrome, competing with whatever A2 decides belongs there.
  • Easy to tune out until it is nearly empty, which is too late for a big import.
B

A page in Settings → Usage

No persistent chip. A dedicated page, in currency first, with a table of recent operations and their cost.

Buys

  • Currency answers "no opaque credits" directly; the complaint about Sudowrite was a number with no stated value, and this page states it every row.
  • Room for real history, which a chip cannot hold.
  • Nothing in the shell to design, test or keep in sync across every screen.

Costs

  • A page nobody visits until the bill surprises them; the exact "invisible spend" SPEC 15 warns warming produces.
  • Converting every credit line to EUR requires a stated, stable exchange rate the product now has to defend and update.
  • No help at the one moment the number should change behaviour: right before an expensive action.
C

Contextual pre-flight only

No persistent chip, no dashboard. Every spend-triggering action shows its own estimate, in both units, right where it is triggered.

Buys

  • The number appears exactly where the decision is being made, the one moment it actually changes behaviour.
  • Showing both units at once retires "opaque credits" without a public exchange-rate promise, since the EUR figure is only ever attached to one concrete action.
  • Nothing to maintain persistently in shell chrome.

Costs

  • A GM who wants to check "how much do I have left" before starting anything has nowhere to look except by starting to spend.
  • The warm budget spends automatically in the background with no click to attach an estimate to; a pure pre-flight design has no moment to show it at all.
  • Many small actions (a portrait here, an ambient layer there) never total anywhere, so slow bleed goes unnoticed; the exact failure SPEC 15 names.

Rejected outright

A single "AI credits" number, and calling any tier "unlimited"

A lone balance with no stated conversion, or copy that reaches for "unlimited" because the routed, capped plan feels generous enough to say it, is precisely what SPEC 15 forbids by name: no opaque credits, never the word unlimited. It also removes the one lever that makes the warm budget legible; if the headline number cannot be tied to a real cost, neither can the budget that spends it while the GM is not looking.

What I would take

A, with C's pre-flight strip added to import and image generation

Take A. The warm budget is the reason this decision is hard, and it is the one number that has no natural click to attach a pre-flight estimate to; it spends on a schedule (SPEC 8.1), not on a request. Only a persistent, ambient meter can make that spend visible without inventing a fake confirmation step for something the GM never asked for. Keep it small: a chip, a bar, nothing that reads as a billing product.

Then add C on top for the two actions large enough to warrant a real choice: an import and a batch image generation both get the confirm-with-estimate pattern, because those are moments where the GM is actively deciding to spend, not just working. B's currency-first framing survives as the destination the shell chip's "what's a credit worth" link opens to, so nothing here is thrown away, only reordered by how often it needs to be seen.

Cheapest way to find out this is wrong: ship the chip alone for one release and watch whether support tickets about "where did my quota go" mention the warm budget specifically. If they still do, the chip is not loud enough and the pre-flight strip needs to move from "before big actions" to "every warm trigger too", which is a real cost this recommendation is betting against.

What this locks in

  • #88 the shell carries two meters, not one; included quota and warm budget are visually distinct so a GM never reads "180 of 600" as part of the same bar as "2,400 of 5,000".
  • #89 the pre-flight pattern from D2 (import estimate) is reused verbatim for image generation in F1, rather than invented twice.
  • #78 the degradation banner copy above ("media first, then drafts, text last") becomes the literal string shown at zero warm budget, not a placeholder for engineering to fill in later.
  • Deferred: a full Usage history page (B's table) still gets built, just as the destination of a link rather than the primary surface. Its exact columns are not decided here.
